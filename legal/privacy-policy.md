# Recurvo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Recurvo helps you track your subscriptions. It reads them from a bank statement you upload, or you add them yourself, and it reminds you before you're charged. This policy explains what information Recurvo collects, why, who helps us process it, and the choices and rights you have.

Recurvo is run by [YOUR FULL NAME], [CITY], Nigeria ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the information described here. You can reach us at cleveraph@gmail.com.

## The short version

- Bank statements you upload are read once to find your subscriptions and are not stored. We keep only the subscriptions you confirm.
- We never keep your full card or account number, only the last 4 digits and the card name.
- We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.
- Analytics never include amounts, merchant names or statement contents.
- You can export your subscriptions or delete your account at any time in Settings.

## What we collect

**Account details.** Your first and last name, email address and password when you create an account, and a profile photo if you add one. Sign-in is handled by our authentication provider, Clerk. We never see your password.

**Your subscriptions.** For each subscription you track: its name, amount, currency, billing cycle, next renewal date, the card it's charged to (card name, last 4 digits and type), whether reminders are on, and when you added or stopped tracking it.

**Your preferences.** Your preferred currency, notification settings, how often you want Smart Reviews, and which Smart Reviews you've chosen to keep.

**Bank statements.** When you upload a statement (PDF or CSV), it is sent securely to our server and to OpenAI, which reads it and returns the card details and recurring charges it finds. Neither we nor our server store the statement: it exists only while it is being read, and the app deletes it from your phone as soon as the scan ends. We keep only the subscriptions you confirm on the review screen. We also keep a short record that a statement was sent (who and when, never its contents) so we can limit each account to 20 statements a day. It is deleted once it is no longer needed, and at the latest when you delete your account.

**Device information.** If you allow notifications, a push token for your device, so we can send you Smart Reviews in future. Renewal and cancel reminders are scheduled on your phone itself and don't involve our servers.

**Usage analytics.** In the released app, we record how the app is used: which screens you open, actions such as adding a subscription or importing a statement, and basic device details such as the model, operating system, app version and approximate location derived from your IP address. These events are linked to your account ID, never to your name or email, and they never include amounts, merchant names, card digits or statement contents.

**Messages you send us.** If you email us, we keep the conversation to help you.

## What we don't collect

We don't collect your full card or account number, your CVV, your bank login details, or your contacts. If you turn on App lock, Face ID or your fingerprint is checked by your phone's operating system. We never receive biometric data.

## How we use your information

- To run Recurvo: create your account, find subscriptions in your statements, save and sync your subscriptions across your devices, convert amounts into your preferred currency, and show insights.
- To remind you before charges, and, if you allow them, to send Smart Reviews.
- To keep the service secure and working: verifying sign-ins, limiting how many statements an account can send each day, and fixing problems.
- To understand how the app is used so we can improve it, through analytics that never include your name, email or financial details.
- To reply when you contact us.

## Legal bases

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and, for users in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the GDPR, we rely on:

- **Contract:** to provide the app you signed up for (your account, statement reading, subscriptions and reminders).
- **Consent:** for notifications, which you can turn off at any time in Settings or your phone's settings.
- **Legitimate interests:** for security, abuse prevention and product analytics. These are kept minimal and never include your financial details.
- **Legal obligation:** where we must keep or share information by law.

## Who helps us (service providers)

We share information only with providers that help us run Recurvo, and only what each one needs:

| Provider | What it does | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| Clerk | Sign-in and account management | Name, email, password, profile photo |
| Supabase | Database and server functions | Your subscriptions, preferences and device push token |
| OpenAI | Reads uploaded statements | The statement, only while it is being read |
| PostHog (EU) | Usage analytics | Usage events linked to your account ID |
| Logo.dev | Shows service logos | The name of a subscription, to look up its logo |
| Expo, Apple and Google | Deliver push notifications | Your device push token and the notification |
| Frankfurter | Exchange rates | Nothing about you: the app downloads public rates |

OpenAI does not use information sent through its API to train its models. It may keep it for up to 30 days to monitor for abuse, and then deletes it.

Some of these providers process data outside Nigeria and the EEA, including in the United States. Where that happens, we rely on the safeguards the NDPA and GDPR require, such as standard contractual clauses and the providers' own data protection commitments.

We don't sell your information or share it for advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others.

## How long we keep it

- Your account, subscriptions and preferences: until you delete your account.
- Uploaded statements: not stored. They exist only while being read.
- Statement rate-limit records: about a day, and never past account deletion.
- Analytics events: as long as they are useful for improving the app, and never linked to your name or email.
- Emails with us: as long as needed to help you.

When you delete your account, we delete your subscriptions, preferences, kept reviews and push tokens from our database, and your sign-in details from Clerk. Copies may remain in our providers' routine backups for a limited time before they are overwritten.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** the information we hold about you.
- **Correct** it. You can edit most of it in the app.
- **Delete** it. Use Settings > Delete account, or email us.
- **Export** it. Use Settings > Export my data to get your subscriptions as a CSV file.
- **Object to or restrict** some processing, such as analytics.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, for example by turning off notifications.
- **Complain** to a regulator: in Nigeria, the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng); in the EEA or UK, your local data protection authority.

To use any of these rights, email cleveraph@gmail.com from the address on your account. We'll reply within 30 days.

## Security

Your data is sent over encrypted connections. Each account can only reach its own data in our database, enforced by row-level security. Statements are never stored. You can also turn on App lock to require Face ID, a fingerprint or your passcode to open Recurvo. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your phone and email account secure too.

## Children

Recurvo is for people aged 16 and over. If you are under 18, you need permission from a parent or guardian to use it. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 16. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
